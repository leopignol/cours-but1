Once upon a time by the sunset, there was a small village of elves, wizards and dwarves in a dark forest.
While the village magicians were fighting a fire-breathing dragon summoned by a wicked witch, everything around them started to fade away.
The dragon let out a scream of agony, magicians saw each other disappear in suffering, the world slowly dismantling.<br/>
On the computer running the simulation, the windows were closing one by one.
A pair of mice were running on the keyboard, pressing random keys, causing the CPU to free every single byte on the memory and turning off the PC.
The pair of mice fled to their hole by the arrival of the PC's owner, and they lived happily ever after.
